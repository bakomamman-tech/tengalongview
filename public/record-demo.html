Record a real TengaLongView run

This browser utility records the live TengaLongView application tab as an unedited WebM video. It cannot fabricate tool calls or simulate a run. The video is capped at 2 minutes 45 seconds to meet the challenge's under-three-minute rule.

  1. Open TengaLongView in a separate browser tab and keep it ready at the beginning of a run.
  2. Click Start recording. In the browser picker, choose Chrome Tab / Edge Tab, select your TengaLongView application tab and share it. Do not choose this recorder tab.
  3. Perform one actual STU-001 Mathematics run; show the MCP event log and the named teacher approval or modification. If time allows, run STU-003 Science to show the insufficient-evidence refusal.
  4. Return to this recorder tab and click Stop & download, or let the 2:45 timer stop automatically. Upload the downloaded .webm video to YouTube or Vimeo and set visibility Public.
